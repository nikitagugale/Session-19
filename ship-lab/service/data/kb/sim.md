# SIM and number

## [lost-sim] A lost or stolen SIM
Block a lost SIM at once by calling 1800-123-4567 or in the app. A replacement SIM is free and arrives in 2 working days.

## [sim-swap] Changing your SIM
A SIM swap needs photo ID at a store. We never ask for your PIN or a one-time code on the phone.

## [port-out] Taking your number to another company
Send PORT and your number to 1900 to get a porting code. The code is valid for 4 days. Leaving may carry exit charges on a term plan.

## [esim] eSIM
An eSIM can be set up in the app in about 10 minutes on a phone that supports it. You need Wi-Fi during setup.
