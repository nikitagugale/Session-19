# Roaming

## [ir-7] International pack IR-7
Pack code IR-7. 1499 rupees for 7 days in 40 countries. 5 GB of data and 100 minutes of calls. Switch it on in the app before you travel.

## [ir-30] International pack IR-30
Pack code IR-30. 3999 rupees for 30 days in 40 countries. 20 GB of data and 300 minutes of calls.

## [uae] Roaming in the UAE
The UAE needs a pack switched on before you land. Without a pack, data is charged at 10 rupees a megabyte. Switch the pack on up to 24 hours before travel.

## [roaming-bill] Roaming charges on your bill
Roaming charges can take up to 3 days to appear on your bill. Charges for an active pack show as one line.
