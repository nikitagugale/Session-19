# Plans

## [up-12] Unlimited Plus 12 (UP-12)
Plan code UP-12. 899 rupees a month on a 12 month term. Unlimited calls, 100 GB of fast data a month and then 2 Mbps, and Wi-Fi calling included.

## [up-24] Unlimited Plus 24 (UP-24)
Plan code UP-24. 799 rupees a month on a 24 month term. Unlimited calls, 100 GB of fast data a month and then 2 Mbps, and Wi-Fi calling included. Leaving before 24 months costs the rest of the discount.

## [basic-5g] Basic 5G (B-5G)
Plan code B-5G. 499 rupees a month, no term. 5G where available, 30 GB of fast data a month, 100 minutes of calls a day, no Wi-Fi calling.

## [prepaid-28] Prepaid 28 day pack (PP-28)
Plan code PP-28. 299 rupees for 28 days. 1.5 GB of data a day and unlimited calls. The pack does not renew by itself.

## [change-plan] Changing or leaving a plan
You can change plan once in each billing cycle. The change starts on the first day of the next cycle. To leave a plan early you pay the remaining months of any discount.
