# Billing

## [due-date] When a bill is due
A bill is due 7 days after it is issued. You can see the due date in the app and on the bill.

## [late-fee] Late fee
A payment more than 3 days late carries a late fee of 100 rupees. The fee is added to the next bill.

## [autopay] Auto-pay
Auto-pay takes the bill amount on the due date. You can switch it off in the app up to 2 days before the due date.

## [dispute] A charge you do not recognise
Raise a dispute in the app within 60 days of the bill. We reply within 5 working days and refunds take 5 to 7 working days.
